# NormWise: Human-in-the-Loop Governance & Review Model

**Document Version:** 1.0.0 (SIH 2024 Final Submission)  
**System Policy:** Assistive Intelligence, Not Autonomous Approval  

---

## 1. Core Governance Principle

> **Fundamental Invariant:** NormWise is an assistive intelligence decision-support tool. It is **NOT** autonomous procurement approval software.
>
> In public procurement, legal responsibility and statutory accountability reside strictly with the authorized government procurement officer and technical evaluation committee. NormWise accelerates information discovery and verifies normative data, but the human reviewer makes the final determination.

## 3. Human Review Workflow Stages

The review workflow is integrated into the primary user journey at `/review` and `/results/:id`:

```text
[ Recommendation Generated ]
             │
             ▼
[ Review Requested ] ──────────► (Officer forwards borderline/sensitive specs to Queue)
             │
             ▼
[ Interactive Checklist ] ─────► (Reviewer verifies Scope, Material Grade, QCO Order)
             │
             ▼
[ Evidence Inspection ] ───────► (Reviewer clicks into Evidence Drawer to inspect clauses)
             │
             ▼
[ Reviewer Notes ] ────────────► (Mandatory contextual rationale recorded by reviewer)
             │
             ▼
[ Formal Review Decision ] ────► (APPROVE / REJECT / REQUEST_CLARIFICATION)
             │
             ▼
[ Audit Persistence ] ─────────► (Decision, Actor ID, Role, and Timestamp logged in PostgreSQL)
```

## 4. Guardrails Enforcing Human Oversight

1. **No Silent Approvals:** A recommendation generated by the engine never enters an "Approved" or "Tender Ready" state without an explicit human action.
2. **Role-Based Authority (RBAC):** Only users with `TECHNICAL_REVIEWER` or `ADMIN` roles can formally sign off on review checklist items.
3. **Segregation of Duties:** A procurement officer cannot approve their own private recommendation if the organization mandates dual sign-off.
4. **Permanent Audit Trail:** Every reviewer decision records the reviewer's unique ID, timestamp, and notes in the PostgreSQL `AuditEvent` table, ensuring complete post-tender auditability for the Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) and vigilance bodies.
