## 1. Step-by-Step Live Presentation Script

1. **Login (0:00 – 0:30):**  
   Log in as `officer@normwise.gov.in`. Present the procurement problem: finding the applicable, current standard from unstructured indents while avoiding obsolete standards and non-compliance.
2. **Input & Extraction (0:30 – 1:00):**  
   Click **"Use Sample Specification"** (Domestic SS Pressure Cooker 5L). Highlight extracted attribute chips: Product, Material (SS 304), Capacity (5L), Safety Valve.
3. **Analyze Requirement (1:00 – 1:45):**  
   Click **"Analyze Requirement"**. Top Candidate appears: **IS 2347:2023** with 92% Match Score (labeled as an internal matching signal).
4. **"Why This Standard?" & Evidence (1:45 – 2:30):**  
   Open the **"Why This Standard?"** drawer. Show score breakdown (Product 30%, Material 15%, Vector 0.88). Open **Evidence Inspector** to display authentic clauses (Clause 4.1 material, Clause 7.2 proof pressure, Clause 8.1 safety valve).
5. **Currentness & Allied Standards (2:30 – 3:15):**  
   Show **Currentness** tab: Active 2023 revision, superseded 2017 revision. Open **Allied Standards** graph: raw material `IS 6911` (SS sheet) and `IS 7466` (gasket).
6. **Statutory QCO Compliance (3:15 – 3:45):**  
   Show **Statutory Compliance** card: Mandatory DPIIT 2020 Quality Control Order requiring ISI mark.
7. **Human Review & Audit Trail (3:45 – 4:30):**  
   Switch to `reviewer@normwise.gov.in`. Complete checklist, enter rationale, and click **"Approve & Finalize"**. Navigate to **Audit Trail** to show immutable SHA-256 event entry.
8. **Closing Value (4:30 – 5:00):**  
   Summarize: Instant discovery, currentness safety, verbatim evidence, QCO compliance, and auditability.
